Badal Tutorial Services

Badal Tutorial Services provides caring, small-class academic support in Kensington, Brooklyn. Families can find Homework Help, K-12 All Subjects tutoring, SAT/ACT and SHSAT preparation, Regents support, ESL, college-level tutoring, and career programs.

Students receive clear instruction, assignment support, practice tests, weekly progress updates, and guidance matched to their goals. Parents can learn about programs, visit the center, request a free assessment, and contact the team by phone, email, or WhatsApp.
